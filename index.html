Who we are

Land knowledge for better decisions.

Solomonic Build Solutions is focused on land surveying and estate development. We help clients understand a site, discuss their development goals and identify the information or professional support needed before moving forward.

We aim to make the process clear: agree the scope, explain the next steps and keep communication straightforward. Construction and specialist trade work are discussed and confirmed separately for each project.

  • Land and site surveying enquiries
  • Site measurement and mapping discussions
  • Estate development planning support
  • Clear project scopes and next steps
  • Construction coordination considered by enquiry
Experience: One year, as provided by Solomonic. Specific qualifications, registrations and statutory authority are not claimed here.

Questions & answers

Before you break ground.

Have a question about where to begin? These answers explain how we approach a new enquiry.

Ask us a question ↗
Can Solomonic help before I buy or develop land?

Yes. You can contact us to discuss the checks and surveying support that may be appropriate before a purchase or development decision. Independent legal advice and official searches should be obtained through the relevant qualified professionals and authorities.

Can I enquire about one service?

Yes. Solomonic's stated focus is land surveying and estate development. Other services shown on this site are by enquiry only; availability, delivery provider and responsibilities must be confirmed before engagement.

Does Solomonic provide legal searches or statutory approvals?

This website does not claim statutory authority or replace independent legal advice. Official searches, legal opinions, permits and approvals must be handled by the relevant qualified professionals and authorities.

What experience does Solomonic have?

Solomonic has provided one year of experience. Contact us to discuss the relevant project scope and the qualifications or registrations applicable to the work. No unprovided credential is claimed here.

How do I get a quotation?

Send the project location, what you need, any available drawings and your preferred timing through the enquiry form or WhatsApp. We will first confirm whether the work is available and who is responsible, then agree a written scope and quotation before work begins.

Are prices or timelines guaranteed on this site?

No. The enquiry packages are starting points, not fixed-price offers or guaranteed availability. A written proposal should confirm deliverables, exclusions, responsible providers, milestones, price and estimated duration.
